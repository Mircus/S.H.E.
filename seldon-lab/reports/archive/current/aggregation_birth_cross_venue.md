# aggregation_birth

## Candidate regularity
Aggregation birth occurs when local closure and sustained activity convert a weak persistence seed into a stable higher-order unit.

## Evidence
Cross-venue support on SDM, WSDM: SDM: aggregation_birth_score (rho=0.2000, events=467); WSDM: aggregation_birth_score (rho=0.2368, events=952)

## Counterexamples
Thin edge seeds with zero closure and weak activity usually fail to become bona fide aggregations.

## Confidence
moderate
